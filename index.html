About

Jared Ramos is a trombonist based in Salt Lake City, UT, where he performs regularly with many different bands and projects.

He received his education in both Utah and New Orleans, earning his degree in commercial music from UVU. Now living in Salt Lake City, Jared performs all over the Intermountain West as a top-call trombonist. In addition to being a full member of multiple bands in town, he also leads his own New Orleans-style brass band, Full Blown Horns, as well as his jazz ensemble, Runaway Collective.
